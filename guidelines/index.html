Community & Safety Guidelines

Effective November 19, 2026 · Megistonia Corporation Limited (Megistonia Corporations)

What gets blocked vs. what gets blurred

ScriptR checks images, video frames, avatars/banners, and link thumbnails in private quarantine before anything goes public:

  • Blocked before publishing: explicit genitalia, full nudity, pornography, and unreadable/corrupted video uploads fail closed and are deleted before anyone else can see them.
  • Blurred as Sensitive Media: milder suggestive content (such as covered swimwear/lingerie or single-region partial nudity below the explicit threshold) is approved behind a tap-to-view blur overlay. You can toggle sensitive-media visibility in Settings.
  • Link thumbnails: unsafe external link thumbnails are stripped while keeping clean text links, or blocked outright on severe violations.

Zero tolerance for child safety violations

Any sexual or indecent upload suspected of depicting a minor (estimated age under 15, blocked SHA-256 or perceptual hash match, or minor-exploitation keywords) is rejected immediately, stored in the blocked-hash list, and records a strike. Borderline apparent-age explicit uploads (15–17) are blocked and queued for review. Accounts reaching 5 strikes are banned automatically.

Harassment, impersonation, and user controls

Argue ideas, not people. Targeted harassment, sharing private personal info, and impersonating someone else are not allowed. Every account can:

  • Report: flag any Script, reply, Loom, message, or account.
  • Block: cut off visibility and direct messages from any account immediately.
  • Mute: filter specific words, phrases, or accounts out of feeds and notifications.
  • Private account & moderation log: require manual follower approval and inspect your own standing, strikes, and notices inside Settings & Privacy.

Urgent safety issues can also be sent directly to scriptsupport.app@gmail.com.
