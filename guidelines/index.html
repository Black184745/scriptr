Community & Safety Guidelines

Effective November 19, 2026 · Megistonia Corporation Limited (Megistonia Corporations)

NSFW filtering and content warnings

Adult or sensitive imagery is flagged by automated NSFW filters and kept behind a blur veil unless turned on in Settings. Use content warnings for spoilers and keep mature media out of general-audience spaces. CSAM/CSAE and illegal material trigger an immediate permanent ban.

Harassment and impersonation

Argue ideas, not people. Targeted harassment, sharing private personal information, and pretending to be another person or organization are not allowed.

Controls available to every user

  • Report: flag any post, reply, Loom, message, or profile for review.
  • Block: cut off visibility and direct messages from any account immediately.
  • Mute: hide specific words, phrases, or accounts from timelines and notifications.
  • Private account: require manual approval for every new follower request.

Urgent safety issues can also be sent directly to scriptsupport.app@gmail.com.
