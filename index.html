A quiet place to write, talk, and follow topics you care about.

ScriptR is a social app built by Megistonia Corporations (Megistonia Corporation Limited). No third-party ad networks, no selling user data.

What is inside the app

  • Scripts and threads: short posts, multi-part threads, replies, quotes, reposts, and polls.
  • Looms: topic communities with moderator tools, flairs, and separate feeds you can keep out of your home timeline if you prefer.
  • Notes: longer essays and reads attached right inside a Script.
  • Messages and voice: direct messages with voice clips, 1:1 calls, and live audio rooms.
  • Your data controls: export your account archive as JSON or delete your account and uploaded media permanently from Settings.

Safety and moderation

Media goes through automated NSFW and safety checks before appearing publicly. Sensitive posts stay blurred behind user-controlled filters, and every account has report, block, mute, and private-follow-approval controls.

Current status: ScriptR is actively in development for iOS and Android.
