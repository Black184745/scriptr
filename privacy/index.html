Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Megistonia Corporation Limited (Megistonia Corporations)

This policy explains what information ScriptR collects, why it is collected, and how you can export or erase it.

1. Data you give us

When you create an account or post on ScriptR, we store your email address, password hash, handle, display name, bio, profile photos, follow graph, notification settings, and the content you publish or send (Scripts, replies, notes, polls, Loom posts, and direct messages).

2. Automated NSFW and safety checks

Uploaded images, videos, audio clips, and reported posts are checked by automated NSFW and safety filters so harmful media can be blurred or quarantined before publication. Private user content is never used to train external AI models.

3. Calls, audio rooms, and notifications

Voice and video calls and live audio rooms route media streams strictly between the participants in that call or room. Device push tokens are stored only to send notifications you choose to receive.

4. Crash reports

Minimal crash diagnostics may be collected to fix bugs and app crashes. Email addresses and message contents are not attached to crash payloads.

5. No ad trackers or data sales

Megistonia Corporation Limited does not sell personal data to brokers and does not include third-party ad tracking SDKs in ScriptR.

6. Exporting and deleting your account

You can download a JSON copy of your account data or permanently delete your account inside the app under Settings & Privacy → Account Security. Deleting your account erases your profile, posts, messages, and uploaded media from active storage.

7. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, write to scriptsupport.app@gmail.com.
