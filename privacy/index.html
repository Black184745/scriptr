Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Megistonia Corporation Limited (Megistonia Corporations)

This policy explains what information ScriptR collects, how automated media screening works, and how you can export or permanently delete your data.

1. Data you give us

When you create an account or post on ScriptR, we store your email address, password hash, handle, display name, bio, profile photos, follow graph, notification preferences, and the content you publish or send (Scripts, replies, notes, polls, Loom posts, voice clips, and direct messages).

2. Quarantine safety screening, blocked hashes, and topic indexing

Image uploads, sampled video frames, profile/Loom images, and external link-preview thumbnails are placed in private quarantine storage before promotion. Automated classifiers run SHA-256 and 256-bit perceptual (PDQ) blocked-hash lookups, nudity/NSFW checks, and apparent-age checks whenever sexual or suggestive signals are present.

Approved photos, video keyframes, and post text also generate compact vector embeddings to match topic feeds. Rejected quarantine files are deleted; for child-safety or borderline-age rejections, we retain the blocked file hashes, strike count, and review signals needed to enforce bans and stop re-uploads.

3. Calls, audio rooms, and notifications

Voice and video calls and live audio rooms route real-time media streams only between active participants in that call or room. Device push tokens are stored strictly to deliver notifications you enable. Voice clips bypass image/video decoders when promoted.

4. Crash reports

Minimal crash diagnostics may be collected to diagnose bugs and app crashes. Email addresses and direct message contents are not attached to crash reports.

5. No ad trackers, data sales, or external AI training

Megistonia Corporation Limited does not sell personal data, embed third-party ad tracking SDKs, or hand your private content over to train external AI models.

6. Exporting and deleting your account

You can download a JSON copy of your account data or permanently delete your account inside the app under Settings & Privacy → Account Security. Deleting your account erases your profile, posts, messages, and media buckets from active storage.

7. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, write to scriptsupport.app@gmail.com.
